# Data and software notices

RAIN PREVIEW displays and processes data from the following sources:

- Rain observation and forecast data: Japan Meteorological Agency,
  [High-resolution Precipitation Nowcasts](https://www.jma.go.jp/bosai/nowc/)
- Basemap data: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan,
  [GSI Maps Vector](https://maps.gsi.go.jp/vector/)
- Map rendering: [MapLibre Native](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-native/),
  distributed under the BSD 2-Clause License

[website terms](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/kishou/info/coment.html).

[GSI Maps terms](https://maps.gsi.go.jp/help/termsofuse.html) and applicable
